About this project

Moodshuffle is a visual inspiration project operated by Grimmy (StupidHimbo) at stupidhimbo.com. It draws real image Pins from the operator's curated Pinterest boards and links each result back to Pinterest. This policy covers /moodshuffle/ and its privacy page, not other tools on this domain.

What Moodshuffle collects

You do not need to register, connect a Pinterest account or submit personal information. These pages do not use application cookies, local storage, advertising trackers or analytics scripts.

When you draw an image, your browser sends the selected category to a Cloudflare Worker. Cloudflare processes technical request information, including IP address and browser details, to operate the service and apply draw-rate limits. The Worker uses the operator's authorized Pinterest connection to read the configured boards and return one randomly selected Pin. It does not maintain a permanent Pin archive.

The browser then requests the chosen image directly from its Pinterest-hosted image URL, so Pinterest or its image-delivery providers may receive technical request information, including your IP address and browser details. Pinterest’s privacy policy applies to Pinterest services.

Pinterest OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in Cloudflare KV until the account is disconnected or authorization is revoked. The Pinterest app secret and administrative key are stored server-side. None of those credentials are included in the public page, browser requests or draw responses.

The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information under its privacy statement. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives technical request information such as your IP address and browser details; see Google Fonts privacy information.

Scope and account controls

The Pinterest integration is read-only: it does not create, change or delete Pins, and visitors cannot access the operator's Pinterest account. The account owner can disconnect through the backend administrative process or revoke access in Pinterest settings. Disconnecting deletes stored OAuth tokens from the Worker KV namespace; revocation prevents further API access. Contact us using the address below for assistance with disconnection or privacy requests.

Sharing and external links

We do not sell personal information or share Pinterest API data with advertising services. Following a link to Pinterest, GitHub or another website takes you to a service governed by its own policies.

Retention and your choices

Moodshuffle maintains no visitor account database or permanent Pinterest data archive. Cloudflare, GitHub, Google and Pinterest apply their own retention policies to the technical information they process. You can browse without connecting an account. If you contact us by email, we use your message and address to respond and handle the request; you can ask us to delete that correspondence, subject to any legal retention obligations.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, email yarrow-hermes-agent@agentmail.to, the project contact inbox. We will update the effective date when this policy changes and revise it before introducing new data collection.
